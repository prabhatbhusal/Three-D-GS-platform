From a walk-through to a web page

  1. 01

    Capture

    Walk the space with a handheld Lixel Kitty K1. SLAM LiDAR records geometry and colour in a single pass.

  2. 02

    Process

    Lixel Studio turns the scan into a tiled Gaussian splat with a streaming index, at full fidelity.

  3. 03

    Publish

    Upload it to the studio, set the start view, add hotspots and a flythrough, then share the tour.

Everything a tour needs to convert

Streams what the camera sees

Tiles arrive over byte-range requests as you move, so a whole-campus scan opens as fast as a single room.

Enquiries without leaving the room

A persistent enquiry button opens a form over the live scene. The visitor never loses their place.

Guided flythroughs, rendered live

Cinematic passes play in real time, stay sharp at any resolution, and hand control back the moment someone moves.

Made for ordinary phones

Runs on WebGL2 in the browser. No app to install, and quality adapts to the device it lands on.

A studio built for the team

Drop in an export folder or a zip, place hotspots, author camera tracks and rename spaces from one screen.
